Sunday, 1 November

Two jobs, about twenty minutes.

One closes a hole that has been letting people book a paid session without paying. The other unlocks the full score breakdown in the Diagnosis & Plan document.

Both are done in Google, from a laptop. Neither touches the website.

1

Update the enquiry autoresponder

5 minutes · do this one first

The button in your auto-reply said Book the introductory call and sent people straight to Cal.com. Payment is collected by Razorpay first, and Cal.com is only where people land afterwards, so that link let anyone who enquired take a ₹339 slot for free. It now points at your booking page instead, which is also where all three prices live, so it will not go stale the next time pricing changes.

  1. Open the Presence Quotient Leads (Responses) sheet, signed in as presence.arpitamehta@gmail.com.

  2. From the sheet's own menu bar: Extensions, then Apps Script. The script editor opens in a new tab.

    It has to be opened this way. Going to script.google.com directly gives you a different, empty project.

  3. In the file list on the left, click enquiry-autoresponder.gs.

    Check you are in the right project before pasting anything: if the tab says Untitled project and the only file is Code.gs with an empty myFunction(), that is a brand new standalone project, not yours. Close it and reopen from the sheet.

  4. Click once inside the code, select all with Cmd+A (or Ctrl+A), delete it, and paste the whole new file I sent you.

    Prefer a smaller change? Find line 51 and replace just that one URL with https://arpitamehta.co/booking. Same result.

  5. Click Save (the disk icon).

That is the whole job. No re-authorising, no trigger changes, no test submission needed. Nothing above line 51 changed, so the emails themselves read exactly as before.
2

Add the four score fields

10 minutes · then send me one link

The assessment already works out all four dimension scores and shows them on the scorecard, but only the overall score, band, strongest and weakest ever reach your sheet. That is the one thing blocking a full score breakdown in the Diagnosis & Plan one-pager. The code to send them is already written and waiting; it needs the four field IDs that only your Form can give.

  1. Open the Google Form behind that sheet, in edit mode, not the responses view.

  2. Add four short answer questions, titled exactly as written here:

    Question titleWhat lands in it
    Score: Image0 to 100
    Score: Executive Presence0 to 100
    Score: Social Culture0 to 100
    Score: Distinction0 to 100

    Leave them all optional. The titles matter because they become your sheet's column headers.

  3. Three-dot menu, top right of the editor, then Get pre-filled link.

  4. Type 50 into each of the four new boxes. Any number works; it is only there so each field shows up in the link.

  5. Click Get link, then Copy link.

  6. Paste that link to me in chat. I will read the four entry.XXXXXXXXXX numbers out of it, put them into the assessment, and show you the change before anything goes live.

Until those IDs are in, nothing changes. The code skips any field with a blank ID, so the assessment behaves exactly as it does today. The sixteen questions and the scoring are untouched, as always.
