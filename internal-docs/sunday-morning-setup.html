5 minutes · do this one first

The button in your auto-reply said Book the introductory call and sent people straight to Cal.com.     Check you are in the right project before pasting anything: if the tab says Untitled project and the only file is Code.gs with an empty myFunction(), that is a brand new standalone project, not yours. Close it and reopen from the sheet.

  4. Click once inside the code, select all with Cmd+A (or Ctrl+A), delete it, and paste the whole new file I sent you.

    Prefer a smaller change? Find line 51 and replace just that one URL with https://arpitamehta.co/booking. Same result.

That is the whole job. No re-authorising, no trigger changes, no test submission needed. Nothing above line 51 changed, so the emails themselves read exactly as before.

3

Verify the domain in Search Console

10 minutes at GoDaddy, then a wait

Search Console is the only place either of us can see whether Google has actually crawled your site, and what people typed to find you. Google is asking you to prove you own the domain by putting a record it can read into your DNS. Your DNS is at GoDaddy, so that is where the record goes.

  1. In the Search Console dialog, click COPY next to the long google-site-verification= string. Do not retype it, one wrong character and it fails with no useful error.

    Then click VERIFY LATER. The property is saved and you can come back to it.

  2. Sign in at godaddy.com. Top right, your initials, then My Products.

  3. Find arpitamehta.co, open it, and go to DNS or Manage DNS.

    One check before you add anything: if that page shows your nameservers pointing at Netlify rather than GoDaddy, stop. The record belongs in Netlify instead, under Domains, DNS records. Tell me and I will walk you through that version.

  4. Click Add New Record and fill it in exactly:

    FieldWhat to put
    TypeTXT
    Name@
    Valuepaste the whole copied string
    TTLleave the default

    The Name must be a single @, which means the domain itself. Not arpitamehta.co, not www.

  5. Add the record. Do not delete or edit any TXT record that is already there, several can coexist, and an existing one may be carrying your email settings.

  6. Go back to Search Console and press VERIFY.

    If it fails, that is normal. GoDaddy usually publishes within minutes but can take a few hours. Leave the record alone and press Verify again later. Deleting and re-adding it only restarts the clock.

  7. Once verified: left menu, Sitemaps, type sitemap.xml, Submit. That is the step that actually tells Google what to crawl.

If GoDaddy turns into a fight, there is an easier route. In the dialog, click try a URL prefix property instead, choose the HTML tag method, and send me the tag. I will add it to the site and deploy, and you verify with no DNS at all. Slightly narrower coverage, same useful data.

Point Topmate and LinkedIn at the site

20 minutes, mostly copy and paste

Worth being straight about what this does. You cannot submit backlinks to Google, and most profile links carry a tag that tells Google not to pass ranking credit. What they genuinely do is three things: they give Google and AI tools a second source confirming who you are and what you do, they get your pages discovered faster, and they send real people who are already interested. The second opinion matters more than it used to, because an AI answering who is a good presence coach in Delhi weighs what other sites say about you, not only what your own site claims.

  1. Topmate. Create the profile if it is not up yet, and put arpitamehta.co in the website field and in the bio text. Mirror your three real tiers so nothing contradicts the site.

    This one doubles as a second bookable storefront, which is why it is first.

  2. LinkedIn, four places, and most people only do the first:

    Contact info, the Website field, labelled Company. Your About section, written out as arpitamehta.co so it is readable even where it is not clickable. The Featured section, add the site as a link with a real title. Your current Experience entry for the practice.

  3. Google Business Profile. You already created it. Check the website field points at arpitamehta.co, and add your services. This is the single strongest one for Delhi searches, because the map pack sits above the blue links.

  4. Instagram bio. One link, pointed at arpitamehta.co/booking rather than the homepage, since that is the page that now does the selling.

  5. Keep the wording consistent across all four: same name, same Presence Consultant, Delhi descriptor, same URL format. Search engines and AI tools match entities on exactly this kind of repetition.

  6. Once Search Console is verified, paste me the list of what you set up. I can then check which links Google has actually found, under Links in the left menu.

What not to do: paid link packages, directory blasts, or anything offering hundreds of backlinks. They are the one SEO activity that can actively get a site penalised, and for a practice selling trust, a spammy footprint is worse than no footprint.
