## Your degree got you the interview. *Your presence builds the reputation.*

Everyone in the building has qualifications. Everyone has completed projects and passed assessments. Very few people know how to create trust, credibility and professional presence on day one, and that is where careers begin to separate.

The graduates who become trusted quickly are rarely the ones who know everything. They are the ones who listen carefully, communicate clearly and conduct themselves professionally, and that is exactly what this playbook is built to teach in the first ninety days.

## Built for the first ninety days.

- **Final-year students** with interviews and placements ahead, who want to walk in already looking like they belong.
- **Freshers** in their first role, learning the unwritten rules in real time, often the hard way.
- **Parents** investing in a child's launch into the working world, not just their degree.
- **Colleges and institutes** preparing a batch of students for corporate life.
