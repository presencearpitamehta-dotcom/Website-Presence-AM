The Programmes

# Four presence consulting programmes. One outcome: *a person the room cannot dismiss.*

Every engagement is private, built around one person, one calendar, one set of rooms. In person in Delhi, online wherever you are, and shaped around your world from the first conversation.

01

## The Presence Edit

Image consulting, done the way a brand identity is built. Your appearance is the first sentence of your story, and until now someone else has been writing it: habit, hurry, an old version of you.

The Edit rebuilds it deliberately. What you wear, how you groom, how you photograph, how you enter. By the end, the mirror and the room finally agree.

Licensed cohort, launched approximately twice a year. Also available as one to one consulting, in person in Delhi or online

![The Presence Edit: image and style consulting](https://arpitamehta.co/images/programmes/presence-edit-v2.jpg)

The Work Covers

- Colour, silhouette and proportion
- Wardrobe: edit, rebuild, buy right
- Grooming and the details people notice
- A style brief any tailor or stylist can run with

02

## The Executive Edge

Executive presence coaching for people who are already good. The promotion, the funding, the client: each one is decided in rooms where competence is assumed and presence is the tiebreaker.

The Edge works on what the room actually reads: voice, posture, pause, the ability to disagree gracefully and to hold attention without raising it.

Licensed cohort, launched approximately twice a year. Also available as one to one consulting, in person in Delhi or online

![The Executive Edge: boardroom presence](https://arpitamehta.co/images/programmes/executive-edge-v2.jpg)

The Work Covers

- Voice, pace, the well-placed pause
- Posture and command of the room
- Meetings, pitches, hard conversations
- Gravitas: being believed, not just heard

03

## The Social Table

Wine, dining and social culture. Careers and alliances are not decided across desks. They are decided across tables, and the table has rules nobody hands you in writing.

The Social Table makes you fluent: the wine list, the toast, the seating, the conversation that flows and the one that should end. Includes a dedicated chapter for brides and couples.

Licensed cohort, launched approximately twice a year. Also available as one to one consulting, in person in Delhi or online

![The Social Table: dining and wine etiquette](https://arpitamehta.co/images/programmes/social-table-v3.jpg)

The Work Covers

- Wine: order, taste, pair, talk about it
- Indian, continental and formal Western settings
- Conversation, toasts, graceful exits
- Hosting: menu, seating, pace

04

## The Debut

Campus to corporate. The first impression of a career is made in the first ninety days, usually by accident. The Debut makes it deliberate.

For graduates and freshers who want to arrive already looking, sounding and behaving like they belong in the building, while their batchmates are still finding the cafeteria. Also delivered as campus workshops.

Licensed cohort, launched approximately twice a year. Also available as one to one consulting, in person in Delhi or online

![The Debut: campus to corporate](https://arpitamehta.co/images/programmes/debut-v2.jpg)

The Work Covers

- Interview presence: enter, answer, ask, leave
- Professional image on a graduate's budget
- Workplace etiquette: email, meetings, hierarchy
- Confidence that reads as readiness
