The Consultant

# The thing nobody teaches you is how to walk into a room and *be believed.*

Arpita Mehta, presence consultant, Delhi. This is the story of why that sentence became a practice.

Arpita Mehta noticed it early. Two people with identical credentials, identical preparation. One got the deal, the promotion, the room. The other did not. And the difference was never the work. It was always the presence.

She spent over ten years in marketing studying exactly that gap: why some people feel premium and others do not, why some brands command loyalty at a price point and others cannot give it away, why the same idea lands differently depending on who delivers it. At some point she stopped applying those insights to brands and started applying them to people.

Now she works with founders, executives, brides, homemakers, and early-career professionals. What they share is not a problem. It is a gap between who they are and how the world currently sees them. Arpita closes it.

> "The most powerful thing you can learn is how to walk into any room and be unforgettable, for the right reasons."

Her practice covers image, executive presence, social culture, and AI for branding and business. It is one of the few in the country that genuinely integrates all of it, because she has not just studied these things, she has lived them. She holds a City & Guilds Certificate in Soft Skills from London, a WSET Level 1 Award in Wines, and is a Certified Image Consultant and Train the Trainer through ICBI. She trained as an engineer, and came up through marketing where her entire job was understanding why people choose what they choose. Her clients arrive accomplished. They leave unforgettable.
